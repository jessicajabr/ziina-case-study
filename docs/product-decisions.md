# Product Decisions

## Core thesis

Landlords need confidence that future rent will arrive and a clear action before a missed payment becomes a manual chasing problem.

## V1 scope

### Build
- Rent schedule
- Risk detection
- Recovery actions
- Payment status visibility

### Do not build
- Underwriting
- Rent-now-pay-later
- Financing
- Guarantees
- Escrow
- Legal collections
- Full property-management CRM
- Ejari integration
- Rewards

## Validation

Test whether landlords can identify an at-risk payment, understand the reason, and choose a recovery action without additional explanation.
